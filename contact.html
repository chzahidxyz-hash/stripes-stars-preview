FAQ

Questions We Get
Before Every First Call.

Honest answers to the most common questions people ask before booking their free audit.

Is the marketing audit really free? What is the catch?+
Yes — completely free, no strings attached. The audit is a 30-minute call where we analyze your current online presence and identify your biggest growth opportunity. There is no pitch at the end, no pressure to buy anything, and no obligation whatsoever. We offer it because we believe that when you see the value of what we do, the conversation about working together happens naturally — and if it does not, you still leave with a clearer picture of your business than when you arrived.
Do I need to sign a long-term contract?+
No. We work on a month-to-month basis for all ongoing services. We do not believe in trapping clients with 12-month lock-ins. The only thing that should keep you with us is the results we deliver every single month — and that is the standard we hold ourselves to. You can cancel any service with 30 days notice, no penalties, no awkward conversations.
How quickly will I see results after we start?+
It depends on the services we start with. Paid advertising (Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Yelp) typically delivers leads within 7 to 14 days of launch. AI systems — the receptionist, chatbot, and follow-up automation — go live and start working the same day they are set up. SEO and GMB optimization build over 2 to 6 months and then compound for years. We usually recommend a combination: one paid channel for fast results, plus the AI and CRM foundation to make sure none of those leads are wasted.
What does the free audit actually include?+
Before your call, we research your Google Business Profile, website, current search rankings, social media presence, and any existing ads. On the call itself, we show you what we found, explain exactly what is working and what is not, and identify the single highest-impact opportunity for your business right now. You leave with a clear action plan — whether you choose to work with us or not.
I have tried marketing before and it did not work. Why would this be different?+
We hear this often — and it is a completely fair concern. Most marketing agencies focus only on getting you traffic and stop there. What usually fails is not the traffic itself — it is the system behind it. Leads arrive, nobody answers fast enough, follow-up never happens, and the lead goes cold. We are different because we build both sides: the marketing to bring leads in, and the AI systems to make sure every single one is captured and converted. That combination is what most agencies are not doing.
Do you work with businesses outside of Austin, Texas?+
Yes — we serve businesses across the entire United States. While we are based in Austin, Texas, all of our services are fully remote-capable. Our AI systems, marketing campaigns, CRM management, and reporting all work location-independently. We have clients in multiple states and serve every industry that benefits from local marketing and AI-powered lead management.
How much does it cost to work with Stripes Stars?+
Our services start from $297 per month for individual services and from $497 per month for packages. Your exact investment depends on which services you need, the scope of your market, and your specific goals. We never present a price before understanding your business — that is what the free audit is for. Visit our Pricing page for starting points, or bring your budget range to the audit call and we will build the right plan around it.
What is the AI Receptionist and how does it work for my business?+
The AI Receptionist is a smart voice system that answers every call to your business number, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including at 2am on Sunday. It greets callers in your business name, answers questions about your services and pricing, handles common FAQs, and books appointments directly into your calendar. Most callers never realize they are speaking with an AI — they just appreciate the instant, professional response. Want to hear exactly how it sounds? Bring that question to your free audit call and we will demo it live for you.
