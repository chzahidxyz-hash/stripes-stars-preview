Overview

Stripes Stars LLC provides marketing, AI automation, CRM, website, and lead-conversion services. When you visit our website, submit a form, book a call, send us an email, or communicate with us by phone or text, we may collect information needed to respond to you, provide services, improve our systems, and operate our business.

This page is a strong business-ready policy draft for your website. Before publishing, you should replace any placeholders with your exact legal business address, support email, and any platform-specific disclosures your attorney or compliance advisor requires.

Information we collect

Information you provide directly

  • Name, email address, phone number, company name, website URL, service needs, budget details, and any other information you submit through contact forms, audit requests, chat, SMS, or email.
  • Appointment details, project requirements, campaign assets, account credentials, billing contacts, and onboarding information you choose to share with us.
  • Communications you send to us, including support messages, call notes, proposals, contracts, and feedback.

Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, referring URLs, time on page, click behavior, and approximate location based on technical signals.
  • Information collected through cookies, pixels, analytics tools, and similar technologies used to understand site performance and marketing effectiveness.

Information from third parties

  • We may receive information from advertising platforms, analytics providers, CRM tools, scheduling platforms, payment processors, or communication providers when you engage with our services or campaigns.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries, schedule consultations, and provide requested services.
  • To create proposals, onboard clients, manage projects, and deliver marketing or automation work.
  • To send operational messages, reminders, invoices, updates, and service-related communications.
  • To improve website usability, diagnose technical issues, analyze traffic patterns, and optimize campaigns.
  • To protect against fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, or other harmful activity.
  • To comply with legal obligations, enforce agreements, and maintain business records.
  • To send marketing or promotional communications where permitted by law or where you have opted in.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information for cash. We may share information only as reasonably necessary in the following situations:

  • With trusted service providers that help us operate our website, CRM, analytics, communications, billing, scheduling, hosting, and project delivery systems.
  • With advertising, messaging, telephony, or automation platforms when needed to execute campaigns or provide the services you request.
  • With legal, regulatory, or government authorities when required by law, subpoena, court order, or to protect our rights and safety.
  • In connection with a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset sale.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

Any third-party provider handling data on our behalf is expected to use that information only for legitimate business purposes and according to their own applicable privacy and security obligations.

Cookies and analytics

Our site may use cookies, pixels, tags, and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure traffic, improve performance, and evaluate marketing campaigns. These tools may collect browser and device data, interaction data, and referral information.

You can usually control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect certain site features or reporting accuracy.

Email, calls, and text messaging

If you contact us, request a consultation, or opt in to communications, we may send you emails, call you, or send text messages related to your inquiry, appointments, account, services, or follow-up. Message frequency may vary depending on your interaction with us.

If you no longer want marketing emails, you can use the unsubscribe link where available. If you no longer want promotional texts, you can reply STOP where supported. Standard message and data rates may apply based on your carrier plan.

Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal, tax, accounting, or operational obligations.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or limitation of certain personal information. You may also have rights to object to certain processing or request data portability.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for business owners and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through our site, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, legal requirements, or business operations. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
