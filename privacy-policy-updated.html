Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Stripes Stars LLC collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit our website or contact us about our marketing and AI services.

Effective Date: June 8, 2026

On This Page

1. Overview

Stripes Stars LLC (“Stripes Stars,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) operates the website stripesstarsllc.com and provides marketing, automation, CRM, and AI-related services for businesses.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through our website, inquiry forms, email, phone, and other direct communications with us.

By using our website or submitting your information to us, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you contact us, request an audit, or inquire about services:

  • Full name
  • Business name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Business category or industry
  • Services needed or requested
  • Monthly marketing budget

We may also collect any additional information you voluntarily provide in messages, emails, forms, or calls.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule consultations
  • Provide proposals, service recommendations, and onboarding support
  • Communicate with leads and clients about requested services
  • Deliver marketing, CRM, automation, and AI-related services
  • Send administrative notices, updates, or service-related communications
  • Improve our website, offers, and customer experience
  • Maintain records for internal business and legal purposes

4. Email and SMS Communications

We may send promotional emails and SMS messages only with your consent. These communications may include updates, special offers, service announcements, or marketing information related to Stripes Stars LLC.

You can opt out of promotional emails by using the unsubscribe link in the email, and you can opt out of SMS marketing by replying with the applicable opt-out instruction provided in the message.

Even if you opt out of promotional communications, we may still send non-promotional messages related to active services, billing, support, or legal matters.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in limited situations, including:

  • With service providers or tools that help us operate our business, such as website, CRM, communication, automation, payment, or scheduling platforms
  • When necessary to provide services you requested
  • To comply with legal obligations, lawful requests, or to protect our rights
  • In connection with a business transfer, merger, or restructuring, if applicable

Any third-party platform you interact with through our services may also have its own privacy practices, and we encourage you to review those policies separately.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational steps to protect the information we collect. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

By using our website or communicating with us online, you acknowledge that you provide information at your own risk.

7. Age Restriction

Our website and services are intended for users who are 13 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has submitted information to us, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to request that we update or correct the information you previously submitted to us.

You may also request that we stop using your information for promotional communication purposes, subject to any legal or operational need to keep certain records.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Your continued use of the website after changes are posted means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.
