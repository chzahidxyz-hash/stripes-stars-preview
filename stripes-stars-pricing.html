PRICING FAQ

Questions About
Our Pricing.

Are the prices shown the final price I will pay? +
The prices shown are honest starting points based on our most common service setups. Your actual price depends on the specific services you need, the scope of work, and your market. Everything is discussed and agreed during your free audit call before any commitment is made. There are zero surprises after you sign.
Do I have to sign a long-term contract? +
No. We work on a month-to-month basis for all ongoing services. We do not believe in trapping clients with 12-month contracts. The only thing keeping you with us should be the results we deliver — and we intend to keep delivering them every single month.
What is included in the free audit? +
The free audit is a 30-minute strategy call where we analyze your current online presence, identify your biggest growth opportunity, and show you exactly what we would build first for your business. You leave with a clear action plan regardless of whether you choose to work with us.
Can I start with one service and add more later? +
Absolutely — and that is exactly what we recommend for most businesses. We identify the single highest-impact service for your situation and start there. Once it is delivering results, we layer in additional services to build your complete growth engine over time.
Are there any setup fees? +
For monthly service packages, there is no separate setup fee — setup is included. For one-time projects such as website development, GHL account setup, or landing page builds, the quoted price is the total price. Everything is fully transparent before we begin.
How do I know if I am getting a good return on investment? +
Every client gets a clear weekly performance report every Monday. We track the metrics that matter — leads generated, calls answered, appointments booked, and revenue attributed. You always know exactly what your investment is producing. If the numbers are not where they should be, we fix it immediately.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit and debit cards, ACH bank transfer, and can set up automatic recurring billing for monthly retainer clients so you never have to think about it. All billing is handled securely through our payment system with invoices emailed automatically.
What happens if I want to cancel? +
You can cancel any monthly service with 30 days notice. No penalties, no cancellation fees, no awkward conversations. We will wrap up any active campaigns cleanly, hand over all assets and access that belong to you, and wish you well. We are confident enough in our results that we do not need a contract to keep you.
