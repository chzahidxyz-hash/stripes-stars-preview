Our Story

in Austin, Texas.

Stripes Stars was founded with a clear frustration in mind — most marketing agencies promise growth but deliver confusion. They run a few ads, send a monthly report full of numbers that mean nothing, and call it a service. Local business owners were being let down, and we knew there was a better way.

"Most agencies stop at getting you leads. We go all the way — from the first Google search to the booked appointment — and make sure nothing leaks in between."

Today, Stripes Stars serves businesses across the United States from our home base in Austin, Texas — combining traditional performance marketing with cutting-edge AI under one roof, so our clients never have to manage multiple vendors again.
